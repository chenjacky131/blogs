Nginx常用命令
----------

by jack at 2022/03/11


##### 1. 查看Nginx安装目录
````
nginx -t
````

##### 2. 查看Nginx实际调用文件
````
ps aux|grep nginx
````

##### 3.重启Nginx
````
// 进入Nginx调用目录，上面第2部
./nginx -s reload
````
##### 4. 查看nginx运行状态
````
 ps  -ef | grep nginx
 或者
 systemctl status nginx.service
````
##### 5. 启动Nginx
````
systemctl start nginx.service
````
##### 6. 停止Nginx
````
systemctl stop nginx.service
````
##### 7. 重载Nginx
````
systemctl reload nginx.service
````
